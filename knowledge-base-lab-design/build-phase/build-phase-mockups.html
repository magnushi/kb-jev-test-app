    How this answers the brief

    The wait gets a horizon

    All nine Sanity stages sit in one line under the map, with queued in front. Done stages are struck through, the current one is underlined. No percentages and no estimates; an elapsed clock is the only time shown, because it's the only time that's true.

    The knowledge core finally exists

    During synthesis the kept chunks cross the Jev line and pack into a core. Each Sanity stage then gets its own motion on that core: reading, grouping, planning, restructuring, writing, checking. A caption says the motion is ours and the stage is Sanity's.

    The wait is framed, not hidden

    One line under the stages: Jev's measured time, the synthesis time, and that Sanity's indexing is the slow part. It repeats the Jev story at the moment people have nothing else to read.

    The reveal is the real outline

    When the build lands, the core fades and lane 3 is replaced by Sanity's outline, rows arriving top to bottom. It's a tree grouped by path, core entries first and heavy, peripheral ones lighter with summaries on hover. related: links draw once on arrival, then only on hover and during answers.

    Core reads as centrality

    No badge. Core entries lead each group, carry weight and a filled marker, and show their summary. A line above the tree quotes the purpose from the left column, so the cause of "core" is visible on the page.

    4 or 40 entries, same 360px

    The outline scrolls inside the map area. Core entries always show; when a group has more than three peripheral entries they fold into "+ N peripheral". The build map stays one click away under "How it was built".

    Answers light up exact entries

    The agent reads entries by path, so the rows it read get the accent tint and a "read" label, and links between them draw. The question it answered sits above the tree.

    Jev and Sanity stay separate

    Jev's lane stops at the divider. Everything after it is labeled Sanity's work, and the ready state says who built the index.
